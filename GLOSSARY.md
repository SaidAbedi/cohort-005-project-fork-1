# Cadence

Cadence sells online courses to Learners. This glossary fixes the language used in the UI, in emails, and in code, so one concept always has one name.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Customer, Buyer, Student, User

**Cadence team**:
The people who run Cadence and review every Refund Request by hand. Always "the Cadence team" in copy, so it is never confused with a Team.
_Avoid_: the team, us, someone, support, staff

### Purchases

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase that buys Team Seats for other Learners.
_Avoid_: Team licence, bulk purchase

**Team Seat**:
One place on a course, bought in a Team Purchase and claimed by a Learner.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's request, with a reason attached, to get back the full amount paid on their Purchase. Every Refund starts as a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The full amount paid on a Purchase, returned to the Learner after their Refund Request is Approved. There are no partial Refunds.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, money back

**Pending**:
The state of a Refund Request that is waiting for the Cadence team to Approve or Decline it.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open, Raised

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request the Cadence team has said yes to. It is the decision only: the Refund follows it, and the Learner's access to the course ends.
_Avoid_: Accepted, Granted, Completed

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request the Cadence team has said no to. No money is returned and access continues.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Refused, Denied

**Withdrawn**:
The state of a Refund Request the Learner took back while it was still Pending.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Retracted

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which the Learner may make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation period

**Watch Limit**:
How much of a course a Learner may have watched and still make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Max watched hours

**Completion Limit**:
The share of a course's lessons a Learner may have completed and still make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Progress cap
